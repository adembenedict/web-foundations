About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a web application that helps you organise your daily tasks and capture ideas without leaving your browser

A clutter free environment that helps you focus on your thoughts and ideas without distractions

How To Use QuickNotes

  1. Open the app in your browser
  2. Click into the input area to draft a new role
  3. Save or organise your notes using built in categories or shortcutss

Features

  • Automatic saving in your local machine and browser
  • Distraction free interface
  • Fast note search and tagging capabilities

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl+N Create a new note
Ctrl+S Save Current note
